# Minimal Device Kit

- 2–4 peristaltic or syringe pumps (feeds)
- Overflow tube or level sensor (constant volume)
- Magnetic stirrer
- Peltier element or heating jacket
- Probe set: pH, ORP, conductivity, dissolved O₂, thermocouple
- Inline UV-Vis flow cell, or LED + photodiode colorimeter
- Optional: camera + LED for colorimetry

Everything speaks USB/serial — scriptable from a single controller.
